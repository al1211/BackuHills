Sections ka sahi order (leather firm ke liye)
Announcement bar + Navbar: free shipping offer, logo, menu, search, cart.
Hero: ek strong photo, ek line ki headline, ek button (“Shop the Collection”). Ye aap bana chuke ho.
Trust strip: 3 ya 4 chhote icons, jaise Genuine Leather, Free Shipping, Secure Payment, Easy Support. Isse turant bharosa banta hai.
Collections / Categories: Belts, Bags, Travel, Western, photo ke saath. Ye bhi ban chuka hai.
Best Sellers: 4 se 8 products, price aur reviews ke saath. Ban chuka hai.
About / Brand story: craftsmanship, asli leather, workshop ki photo. Ban chuka hai. Ye section log sabse zyada yaad rakhte hain.
Why choose us ya process: leather kaise banta hai (hide chunna, cutting, stitching, finishing), 3 se 4 steps mein. Isse “premium” feel aata hai.
Customer reviews: 3 se 6 asli reviews, naam aur photo ke saath. Ye sabse zyada bikri badhata hai.
Instagram / lifestyle gallery: log belt ya bag pehne hue. Isse brand zinda lagta hai.
FAQ: shipping, return, leather care. Ban chuka hai.
Newsletter + Footer: signup par kuch offer dein (jaise 10% off), aur policies ke links, contact, social links.

Aapke paas abhi 4 se 5 sections hain. Sabse zyada fark Trust strip, Reviews, Process aur Gallery jodne se padega.
